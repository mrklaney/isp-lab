ISP Lab

Runnable notebooks on Jacob A. Barandes's indivisible stochastic processes. Each page opens in the browser; nothing to install.

These notebooks are a computational companion to Barandes's papers. They illustrate and test his results, and credit his work and others' wherever an idea comes from them. They do not claim new physics.

Notebooks

  1. Stochastic processes (study copy)
  2. Divisibility (study copy)
  3. Indivisibility (study copy)
  4. Quantum emergence (study copy)
  5. Interference (study copy)
  6. Division events (study copy)

The study copies add long-form explanation, derivations and quoted passages from the papers.

Sources

  • Paper 1: "The Stochastic-Quantum Correspondence", arXiv:2302.10778 (Philosophy of Physics 3(1), 8, 2025).
  • Paper 2: "The Stochastic-Quantum Theorem", arXiv:2309.03085.
  • Paper 3: "Quantum Systems as Indivisible Stochastic Processes", arXiv:2507.21192 (Stud. Hist. Phil. Sci. 119, 102213, 2026).

Code, tests and the full reference list: github.com/mrklaney/isp-lab. Equations render with MathJax and need an internet connection.
